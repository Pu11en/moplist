## The one argument (use this everywhere)

Competitors sell **volume**. AlphaLeads advertises ~5,400 filings per day.
Our own live run shows what volume contains:

```
298 new filings → 19 with real commercial premises
                  45 houses · 3 UPS Store mailboxes · 83 unusable
```

**93% of new business filings are worthless to a cleaner.** Nobody else filters
for "does this place have floors, restrooms, and a door someone unlocks."

> **They send you 5,400 filings a day. We send you the 19 with a floor to mop.**

## Objections (updated)

| They say | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I can get this cheaper" | True — raw filings are ~$20/mo. But 93% of that is houses and mailboxes. You're paying $49 to not sort 5,000 rows. |
| "Are these qualified?" | **No, and don't pretend otherwise.** They have premises. They have not said they want a cleaner. It's a first-mover list, not an inbound lead. |
| "Are they even open?" | Filing date ≠ opening date. Some are pre-open — which is the point, they need a cleaner before move-in. |
| "$49 for a list?" | Their own numbers: $400 on Thumbtack produced one job. Let that sit; don't argue. |
| "Can I try it?" | The 3 in this email are free and already delivered. |

## Do not claim

- That leads are qualified, interested, or ready to buy.
- Any conversion rate. We have none.
- That we verify the business is trading — we verify the *address type*, nothing more.
- Any customer count or testimonial. There are zero customers.

## CAN-SPAM basics (for Instantly setup)

Real sender name, real physical mailing address in the footer, working opt-out
("reply stop" counts if honoured), no misleading subject lines. Honour opt-outs
immediately and permanently.
